Rounded push-fit · inside or outside

Start with a base and lid as separate bodies in their closed positions. Choose two matching vertical walls, then their straight seam edge. The rounded bump and recess are added together; a flexible arm allows push-on, pull-off closure.

Compact lid preset: 6 mm wide, 1.2 mm thick, 12 mm reach, 0.45 mm bump engagement and 1.25 mm nose radius; two equally spaced snaps. It only applies when you press the button. These are starting dimensions, not a guarantee of fit or opening force. Test a small sample in your material.

Do not select: the horizontal box rim, lid underside, or one inner wall and one outer wall. The blue faces in the diagrams are the vertical faces to select.

Examples & help

Select one rectangular face of a uniform solid plate, then Done. Choose Warren, Pratt or Howe, Bays, Member width, Frame width and Long/Short side direction. The preview removes triangular windows through the original thickness. Create truss confirms the same body; Saved → Edit loads its saved settings. This is a geometry helper, not a strength calculation.

Through holes cut the selected wall. Engrave removes a shallow pattern; Emboss adds raised details. Flat colour fills separate material regions flush with the original surface. Export its two parts as 3MF and assign filaments in Bambu Studio. Grilles provide planar parallel, crossed or inclined openings. Fill face repeats your Cell size with the chosen Minimum gap and Border. Partial cells are clipped at the border by default. Tap ? beside any setting for an illustrated explanation.
